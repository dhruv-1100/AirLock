and is outside OpenShell's enforcement scope entirely; no policy engine
constrains a host process. The claim there is architectural, not enforced: the
inspect service binds 127.0.0.1 only, links no cloud SDK, and reports
bytes_egressed: 0 on every verdict. The ethernet unplug is the host-side proof.
Do not blur the two.

One deviation from SRS S2.5 worth stating: the provider base URL is the docker
bridge IP 172.17.0.1:8000 rather than host.openshell.internal, because that name
does not resolve where the CLI runs its verification. Same host, same listener,
still off the LAN.
